Improved weather forecast for Budva using machine learning
11 February 2026

I took 6 global weather models and let machine learning extract a more accurate forecast for Budva from their errors. The result is better than any single model.

## Why one forecast isn't enough

None of the 6 global and continental models available to the general public (myself included) can be the best at everything. But when you put them together and let a machine learn from their errors, you can get a much smaller error.

## XGBoost gradient booster

[XGBoost](https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/) is an algorithm that takes data and produces a coefficient matching the range and magnitude of the error. I gave it:

- **6 models** as input (ECMWF, GFS, ARPEGE, ICON, METEO FRANCE and ITALIAMETEO)
- **9 parameters**: temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, cloud cover, precipitation and a couple more
- **~3 years of data** from a local Weather Underground station in Budva with all the parameters above + solar radiation

Result: a temperature MAE of **0.97°C**. Less than one degree of error!

The best single model has an error of 1.4°C. My frankenstein of 6 models is **30% better**.

## Things I learned the hard way

- **Feature engineering is 80% of the work**: hour of the day, month, lag features...
- **Bias correction by month and hour**: when you discover that ECMWF reads 2°C too high *every January*, the correction is trivial but the effect is huge. The same goes for any other situation.
