How I spent a month and a half building a parking website (and why)
1 February 2026

It took me **a month and a half**. Working time, not calendar time. And no, it's not just a landing page...

[Parkiranje.me](https://parkiranje.me).

## What is Parkiranje.me anyway?

In short: a platform where you can find, book and pay for a private parking spot in Montenegro. Airbnb for parking, if you like.

**For drivers**: You enter a location, pick a date, see what's available, pay online, get a confirmation. Done. No circling the block, no "hey, is there a spot", none of those 30-40 euro fines because you parked where you shouldn't.

**For owners**: Got an empty driveway? A yard? A garage serving as storage for things you'll throw out "one day"? List the space, set a price, and start earning while you sleep.

> The average driver in Montenegro loses **19 hours** a year looking for parking and spends **around 100 euros** on fines.

## A month and a half — where the time went

I started out optimistic. "Two weekends and it's done."

### Weeks 1-2: The basics
Map, location search, parking spot cards. Basic authentication. It looks easy written in one sentence, but a map that works properly on mobile... that's a special kind of hell. SUPABASE! Don't even mention SUPABASE to me! Maybe the worst configuration for PostgreSQL, but now that I've built it I'm not changing it...

### Week 3: Bookings
The booking system — pick a date, pick a time, check availability, create a booking. Sounds simple. It isn't. Timezone problems, overlapping bookings, edge cases that wake you up at 3 in the morning.

### Week 4: Payments
Online payments. Because of course it can't just be "give me cash when you get here." I had to integrate a payment gateway, make it work, and test it with real cards.

### Weeks 5-6: Everything else
A dashboard for owners. Notifications. FAQ. Privacy policy (copied off the internet, I won't lie). Responsive design for mobile. Testing. Bugs. More bugs. "How does this work in production but not locally" moments.

And finally the deploy, the domain, SSL, and a prayer to the atheist god that everything works.

The plan is to expand across all of Montenegro, and maybe further. Who knows.

## Current state

The site is in **beta**. It has a test parking listing and the system works, but I haven't started real user acquisition yet. That's the next phase, and probably harder than the programming itself.

Because building a site is one thing. Convincing people to put their parking space on the internet... that's a separate story that needs attention, and before the season.
